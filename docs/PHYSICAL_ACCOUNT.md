## What would change if the vessel had one single platform modification (mast_4)?
Closing question: we would add one more guest mast slot, mast_4 (+1 slot: <= 60 kg, <= 0.6 m2, +2,000 W forced air). Today G1 (1,500 W) and the G5 antenna (1,200 W) cannot share mast_3 (2,700 W > 2,000 W), so the antenna goes on the hangar roof and every G4 recovery depends on a roof-clearance condition. With mast_4 the antenna moves off the roof (9,853 -> 9,898 kg-eq, still 2,102 kg-eq margin), the drone conflict disappears and D1/D3 lose a conditional rule. It does not fix mass: {G1,G2,G3,G4} (10,035 kg), {G1,G2,G3,G5} and {G2,G3,G4,G5} still fail. The same gain applies to {G1,G2,G4,G5} (7,853 -> 7,898 kg-eq).
