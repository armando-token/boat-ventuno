## What would change if the vessel had one more mast position (mast_4)?
Closing question: we would add one guest mast slot, mast_4 (<= 60 kg, <= 0.6 m2, 2,000 W air). Today G1 (1,500 W) and the G5 antenna (1,200 W) cannot share mast_3 (2,700 > 2,000 W), so the antenna sits on the hangar roof and every G4 recovery depends on a roof-clearance condition. With mast_4 the antenna leaves the roof (9,853 -> 9,898 kg-eq, 2,102 kg-eq margin left), the drone conflict disappears and D1/D3 lose a conditional rule. It does not fix mass: {G1,G2,G3,G4} (10,035 kg) still fails.
