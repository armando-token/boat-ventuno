## 3. Scenario B (`G1+G3+G4+G5`) Power, Feeder, Water & Air Ledger Across Transitions
- **Electrical Power (`actual / committed`)**: Initial `57.5 / 81.5 kW` (`G1=1.5`, `G3=1.0` actual / `25.0` firm charge, `G4=10.0`, `G5=45.0`) -> `82.5 / 106.5 kW` at `T=480 s` when `G4` `35.0 kW` recovery/charge is reserved -> at `T=720 s` (`100 kW` generator reserve), `106.5 kW > 100 kW` triggers `G5` step-down (`45.0 -> 8.0 kW`), yielding `45.5 / 69.5 kW <= 100 kW` (`30.5 kW` committed margin).
- **Chilled-Water & Air Domains**: Water load is `54.8 / 78.8 kW` initial -> `79.8 / 103.8 kW` with `G4` `35 kW` -> `43.5 / 67.5 kW <= 72 kW` after `G5` step-down (`43.8 -> 7.5 kW` water), surviving `30 deg C` + `-40%` loop loss (`72 kW`). Air cooling is `1,500 W` on `mast_3` (`G1`) + `1,200 W` on `hangar_roof` (`G5` antenna) = `2,700 W` initial (`2,000 W` after `G5` step-down to `500 W` roof air).
- **Feeder Routing (`<= 150 kW/line`)**: `LINE_A_FWD = 2.5 / 26.5 kW` (`G1` `1.5 kW` + `G3` `1.0 / 25.0 kW`); `LINE_B_AFT = 80.0 -> 43.0 kW` (`G4` `35.0 kW` + `G5` `45.0 -> 8.0 kW`).

## 4. What would change if the vessel had one single platform modification (`mast_4`)?
If the vessel added one second mast slot `mast_4` (`<= 60 kg, <= 0.6 m2, <= 2,000 W` independent forced-air cooling, `x3.0` stability factor), **before vs. after solver results** change as follows: for `{G1, G3, G4, G5}` (`9,560 kg`) and `{G1, G2, G4, G5}` (`7,560 kg`), `G5_MAST_ANTENNA` (`25 kg`, `0.3 m2`, `1,200 W` air) no longer collides with `G1` (`45 kg`, `0.5 m2`, `1,500 W` air) on `mast_3` (`2,700 W > 2,000 W`) and no longer requires `hangar_roof` placement or conditional `G4` rotor-clearance verification (`ROOF_OUTSIDE_G4_RECOVERY_V1`), moving from `9,853.0 kg-eq` (`hangar_roof`) to `9,898.0 kg-eq` (`mast_4`, `2,102.0 kg-eq` margin) with unconditional static placement (`YES` at port; `YES_IF` under 14-day Mission Envelope A.10 solely for `W4` EMCON offline buffering and `W2`/`W3`/`W5` time-multiplexed windows). However, mass (`10,000 kg`), bay capacities (`bay1 <= 8,000 kg`, `bay2 <= 4,000 kg`), and stability (`12,000 kg-eq`) still limit the other three 4-guest combinations: `{G1,G2,G3,G4}` (`10,035 kg`), `{G1,G2,G3,G5}` (`12,070 kg`, `12,210 kg-eq`), and `{G2,G3,G4,G5}` (`13,015 kg`, `13,263 kg-eq`, plus `G2+G5 = 6,500 kg > 4,000 kg` in `bay2`).
